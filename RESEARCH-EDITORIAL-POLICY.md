# House of Ngwedi Research & Editorial Policy

## Ancient texts
Each catalogue entry should identify, where known: earliest surviving witness; language; manuscript tradition; approximate date range; community/canon context; modern scholarly uncertainty; and a clearly labelled spiritual or comparative reading. Do not describe a work as "forbidden" or "suppressed" without specific historical evidence.

## Current affairs and trade
The Global Affairs & Trade page is a discovery and briefing service. Automated collection uses GDELT public article metadata and runs every six hours through GitHub Actions. Metadata is not an endorsement or verification. Editors should confirm major claims against original government, multilateral, court, company or statistical releases and at least one independent reputable report before publishing analysis.

## Correction and transparency
Keep publication dates, source names and canonical links. Distinguish reporting, official statements, opinion and House analysis. Correct material errors with a visible update note. Do not silently change historical entries.

## Respect
Treat African oral traditions, sacred knowledge and living communities with respect. Seek permission before publishing restricted or community-held knowledge. Do not flatten distinct African cultures into one undifferentiated tradition.

## Safety and advice
No medical diagnosis, guaranteed healing, investment recommendation or legal advice. Explain uncertainty and direct readers to qualified professionals where appropriate.
